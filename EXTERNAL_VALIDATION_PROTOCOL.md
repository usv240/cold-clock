# External validation protocol

Do not invent users, quotes, or outcomes. Run one 20-minute synthetic tabletop with a pharmacist, pharmacy technician, emergency-preparedness lead, or medication-access coordinator.

Ask the participant to complete the outage-to-receipt workflow without coaching, then record: role (not personal identity), completion time, wrong turns, safety concerns, whether the human medication gate was clear, and one product change they require. Obtain permission before publishing any quote.

Acceptance target: 4/5 or better clarity, no mistaken belief that AI made the disposition, and completion without developer intervention. Put dated, consented evidence below only after the session.
