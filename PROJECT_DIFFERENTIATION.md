# ColdClock: Prior Art and Differentiation

ColdClock does not claim the surrounding categories are empty.

| Existing category | Existing capability | ColdClock's bounded contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Home medication monitoring | Sensors, temperature history, thresholds, and alerts | Turns observed evidence into a persistent resolution case |
| QChainMED research system | Continuous monitoring from dispensing through domestic storage | Adds professional routing, approved fulfillment, delivery, and receipt closure |
| Medical refrigerator systems | Local and remote temperature/power alarms | Patient-side evidence and cross-organization coordination |
| Professional excursion references | Product lookup and pharmacist assessment | Assembles the exact patient-side facts needed for a named reviewer |
| Medication-management platforms | Adherence and pharmacist-led services | Starts from a temperature event and enforces a disposition gate |
| Pharmacy delivery | Fulfillment and last-mile delivery | Unlocks those actions only after excursion review |

The contribution is therefore not “AI knows whether insulin is safe.” That would be both unsafe and
weakly differentiated. The contribution is a source-bearing, human-authorized resolution loop.
